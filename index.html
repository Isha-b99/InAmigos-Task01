About the Foundation

A Section 8 nonprofit built on collective action

InAmigos Foundation was founded on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, Founder & CEO. It is a Section 8 registered non-profit organisation, licensed by the Central Government and headquartered in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, operating with a network of professionals and volunteers across India.

Our Mission

To bring positive change through food distribution, education, women's empowerment, animal welfare and environmental action.

Our Vision

A more inclusive, compassionate and empowered society, driven by collective action rather than by any single institution.

Ongoing Initiatives

Six projects, one mission

Each initiative targets a specific, verified gap in society — from an empty plate to an unplanted sapling.

Project Seva

The problem: many families in and around Bilaspur go without reliable meals or warm clothing.

Seva distributes food and clothing directly to underprivileged communities, serving humanity one meal at a time through volunteer-led drives.

Project Bachpanshala

The problem: underprivileged children fall behind on school support and basic digital literacy.

Bachpanshala bridges the educational gap by teaching basic digital literacy, life skills and school education support to children who need it most.

Project Jeev

The problem: stray and vulnerable animals across the region lack consistent feeding and care.

Jeev is dedicated to animal welfare — rescue, protection and daily feeding of 50+ stray animals through a committed volunteer network.

Project Udaan

The problem: women in rural areas often lack access to income-generating skills and financial independence.

Udaan partners with self-help groups in rural areas to build financial independence, practical skills and menstrual hygiene awareness.

Project Prakriti

The problem: shrinking green cover and unsustainable farming practices threaten local ecosystems.

Prakriti advocates for sustainability by planting saplings — 20,000+ to date — and supporting eco-friendly agriculture.

Project Vikas

The problem: young people frequently lack access to real-world, hands-on professional experience.

Vikas has trained 30,000+ interns over four years across data operations, finance, research, content, digital marketing and social work.
